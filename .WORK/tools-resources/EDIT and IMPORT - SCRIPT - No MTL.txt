INTRO (PROLOGUE STORY ROLL)
============================================================

London, capital of the British Empire,
late 1800s.

Awash in outlandish tales, dastardly deeds by
unseen foes, citizens vanishing abruptly, and
rumbling deep within the earth, a dark
malaise permeates throughout this city.

Now, under the silent cloak of darkness in a
corner of this misty capital, those who crawl
the earth begin their clandestine wriggling
once again...

Nabokov, an ingenious scientist, fled from
the collapsing Russian Empire and resettled
with his family here in London.

He soon noticed London's troubles and
determined the source that tied all these
turbulent trends in the dead of night
together.

Bringing his intellect and talents to bear,
along with the help of Mikhail's sorcery, he
set out to put right what he found wrong in
the world.

Alas....
