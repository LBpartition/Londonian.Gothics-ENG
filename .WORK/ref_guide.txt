LONDONIAN GOTHICS: A Lolita in the Labyrinth             ~Notes by SakuraSylph~
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~               v.27 May 2018

Released as Megacyber's debut title for the Nintendo DS in 2005, Londonian
Gothics describes itself on the case as a gothic-lolita action-RPG.  The former
half refers to an elegant fashion style based upon Victorian (England) and
Rococo (France) era garments full of lace and frills, with gothic-lolita
dresses plus crafting culture being featured prominently as part of the
gameplay.  The latter half refers to the dungeon-crawling main structure of
the game.  In sum, Londonian Gothics' gameplay could be described as a
combination of Blizzard's PC title Diablo and the classic game Pac-Man.  The
details will become clearer later on, but for now that's the basic idea of what
you can expect.

Some folks seem to get confused by this game's subtitle, because one of its
words has an unrelated, and unfortunate, connotation in US English.  To
explain, someone who wears gothic-lolita fashion, as this game's protagonist
does, is called a Lolita.  And that is what the subtitle refers to.  Nothing
else.

Londonian Gothics was never released outside of Japan, so there's not a lot of
information about it available online.  Even within Japan physical copies are
a bit rare but can be obtained on the second-hand market inexpensively when
found.  This guide includes a translated script so even if you don't know
Japanese you can still enjoy the plot as it unfolds.

Megacyber was purchased by Konami only a few months after Londonian Gothics
came out, so L.G. ended up being Megacyber's last and only DS game too, not
just its first.  A planned sequel to Londonian Gothics on the DS never
materialized, but a doujin spin-off eventually did in 2011.  It's called
Lolita of Labyrinth, and while it's a different type of game and has all
new characters, it was very clearly intended to be in the same universe as
Londonian Gothics.  The spinoff is available on the Japanese Xbox Live Indies
store for 309 yen.

That concludes the short history lesson on what Londonian Gothics: A Lolita
in the Labyrinth is all about and where it came from.  On with the show!

I. BACKGROUND INFORMATION
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Story Setting:
--------------
Alice's father has been kidnapped by creatures of the night, and it is unknown
whether he's alive or dead.  You, as Alice, insist on following down into the
labyrinth via a hole in the basement to find and rescue him.  Your lolita
dresses contain the powers needed to protect you from the dangers that lurk
within.  (Game's tagline: "It has to be me!" / watashi ja nakya dame na noo!)


Title Screen:
-------------
 -New Game
 -Load
 -Dress Mode : Admire lolita dresses collected, cumulative across saves.
 -Options


Options Screen:
---------------
 -Music Volume   : 1=mute, 2-5 higher is louder.
 -Effects Volume : 1=mute, 2-5 higher is louder.
 -Text Speed     : 1=(slow + manual advance); 2=(slow + autoadvance);
                   3=(fast + autoadvance).
 -Backlight : on / off (warning: "off" blanks the screen on 3DS).


Characters:
-----------
 -Alice   : You, the player.  Comfortably raised in a high-class family that
            valued inquisitiveness, Alice is gifted with crimsonlake eyes and
            long blonde hair down to her waist.  However her behavior isn't
            as refined as one might expect, as she's headstrong and charges
            into things without thinking them through first.

 -Mikhail : The manor's butler.  Also a sorcerer hailing from the Scottish
            highlands.  Always level-headed, polite, and deferential, it's
            hard to read the difference between his serious statements and his
            jesting ones.

 -Lucille : The manor's talented maid, who singlehandedly manages the affairs
            of the large residence.  However, toxicity lurks within.  Her ego
            makes her prideful, and she is known to say snarky and callous
            things.  Nicknamed the toxi-maid.

 -Nabokov : Alice's father.  A grieving scientific genius who is no fan of war
            or malice, he was driven out of his native Russia by civil unrest
            in the declining years of that empire.  Now in London performing
            experiments combining science with sorcery, he was kidnapped by
            disciples of "the old gods who wander eternity" and taken to the
            deepest part of the underworld labyrinth.  He burns with a strong
            sense of justice and frequently gets absorbed in his research,
            making him easily misunderstood by those around him.

 -Additionally you can interact with certain monsters within the labyrinth to
  obtain hints or purchase items.


Japanese Fandom Memes Trivia:
-----------------------------
* "Kyaa, be be boh!" - Alice's scream followed by the three notes of the death
  fanfare.  You'll probably hear this a lot.  It became a meme.  People would
  race to complete the phrase on forums, one poster writing "Kyaa!", while
  the next poster would try to slip in "Be be boh!" before anyone else wrote
  another message.  Also, fan doujin books were titled "Be Be Bon" after this
  meme, with the alternate reading of hon (book), bon, replacing boh since
  the fanbook was of course a book.
* "xxx ja nakya dame na noo" - Riffing on the game's tagline in Londonian
  Gothics related online areas.  For instance, the 2ch board changed its usual
  "Anonymous" usernames to "nanashi ja nakya dame na noo" ("I've got to be
  Nameless!").

II. CONTROLS AND GAMEPLAY
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Controls:
---------
 L : Rotate Alice to her Left by 90 degrees, without moving any squares.
 R : Rotate Alice to her Right by 90 degrees, without moving any squares.
 D-Pad : Move in the direction pressed, one square. (or hold to walk.)
 Start : Confirm choice on any menu screen.
 A : Confirm choice on any menu screen; trample upon monsters;
      Hold to manually speed up text display as desired if on setting 1 or 2.
 B : Cancel out of any menu; hold to dash with the d-pad.
 X : Pause game and enter the menu.
 Y : Use the power of the currently-equipped lolita dress.
 L+R+Start+Select : Soft reset.


Basic Gameplay Flow:
--------------------
On each floor of the labyrinth, the monsters are invulnerable at first and will
one-hit-kill Alice.  Alice must:
 1. Avoid the monsters via any available method, while searching for the exit
    to the current room.
 2. Some rooms lock upon entry; step on all magic circles in the room in order
    to unlock the doors and make the room exitable.
 3. One room will have different music, and this room houses a huge pulsing red
    and black orb called a Bordermoon; destroy this by touching it.
 4. The music changes and Alice becomes the one invulnerable.  Step on each
    monster's back and once it falls to the floor, repeatedly kick (A) to
    defeat it, earning an item, money, or dress pattern.
 5. Take appropriate patterns and items into a sewing room, found throughout
    the levels, and craft new lolita dresses in order to increase the variety
    of powers (e.g. shrink, teleport, scouting) available for use.
 6. While the music is still changed, go through the exit down to the next
    level of the labyrinth.

That is the basic outline on how to handle a level, but in practice how you
play is up to you.  You can beat up all the monsters, or just some of them, or
even the same ones repeatedly as they respawn upon reentering a room.  If a
monster drops a dress pattern, then it will always be the first thing they
drop, so I would recommend defeating each monster on every floor at least once
in order to find all the dresses.  If you run out of time while monster
stomping then the Bordermoon orb and all magic circles in the level respawn.
No problem; just go destroy the Bordermoon again and resume.

There are two main types of safe rooms throughout the labyrinth: sanctuaries
(marked by silver arches) and sewing rooms (marked by silver columns).  The
former will replenish your stock of lives, refill your DP meter, and allow
you to warp back to the manor.  The latter lets you craft dresses.


Lower Screen:
-------------
* Roses : Number of Lives remaining.  Starts at 5.
* Magic Circles : Number of active Magic Circles remaining in current room.
* RGB heartbeats show each enemy's distance from Alice.  Higher = closer.
* Red meter is time remaining until Bordermoon orb returns & resets the level.
* Purple meter is your DP.  Your stamina for running & using dress powers.
   -This refills automatically over time.
   -If it hits 0, the bar turns silver and you can't run or use powers until
    the meter refills far enough to turn purple again.
* Dress : Change to a different outfit and power.  For uncrafted dresses, view
          which items are needed in order to make it.
* Item : View money and currently-held inventory items.  Can discard items.
* Save : Save game.  Available anytime.


Gameover Consequences:
----------------------
 Restart at the most recent sanctuary visited.  All Bordermoons and magic
 circles respawn.  However, you keep all items, dresses, and money obtained.


Further Factoids:
-----------------
* Be sure to enter all sanctuaries at least once in order to unlock those
   floors for warping.
* Time is paused during outfit changes, so they can be done in emergencies.
* Inventory is capped at only six (6) items.  Picking up a seventh will ask you
   whether you want to discard one existing item or cancel the pickup.
* Monsters nearby where a dress pattern is found are more likely to carry the
   items needed to craft it.
* Enemies respawn every time you reenter a room.
* Magic Circles don't reset once the room unlocks for the first time though;
   they stay unlocked until the Bordermoon respawns after being destroyed.
* Bordermoon state on current level is retained upon loadgame as long as the
   save was created within the level.  Loads from saves within a sanctuary or
   sewing room will not retain any Bordermoon or magic circle data (all will
   be reset).

IV. STORY SUMMARY AND GAME WALKTHROUGH (spoiler-full)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

[00].Prologue Story Roll (on bootup):
-------------------------------------
London, capital of the British Empire, late 1800s.  Awash in outlandish tales,
dastardly deeds by unseen foes, citizens vanishing abruptly, and rumbling deep
within the earth, a dark malaise permeates throughout this city.

Now, under the silent cloak of darkness in a corner of this misty capital,
those who crawl the earth begin their clandestine wriggling once again...

Nabokov, an ingenious scientist, fled from the collapsing Russian Empire and
resettled with his family here in London.

He soon noticed London's troubles and determined the source that tied all
these turbulent trends in the dead of night together.

Bringing his intellect and talents to bear, along with the help of Mikhail's
sorcery, he set out to put right what he found wrong in the world.  Alas....

Varuudanu Fight:
----------------
The boss has a repeating attack pattern.  You can tell it's about to launch
one of the below-mentioned volleys when it raises its arms.  It does these
three attacks, usually in order, with pauses to walk around in between.
Successfully hurting it with the holy sword will make it start over from the
first attack type.

A: Fires about 4 beams in straight lines from its belly, toward wherever
   Alice is standing.
B: Breathes cloud-like projectiles that spiral outward toward Alice.
C: Punches with its arms three times.

Just like in the game, one hit means death.  As you must wear Argus, the
teleportation-on-touch dress Iscarpe is not available.

Alice slashes with the sword via the Y button.  Fortunately, you can run and
attack at will without draining any DP.  Damage the boss by chopping it in the
shoulder, from the side.  Eleven (11) hits in a row will kill the boss and
finish the game.

The basic strategy is to hang out near the boss and dodge its projectiles,
while trying to get around the side of it (without it immediately turning to
face you).  The boss will change directions only when it is standing still,
so once it starts moving in a particular direction, that's when you quickly
flank it.  It moves only about three squares forward before stopping again
to unleash an attack and then turn, so you have to either be fast, or once
you get used to its movement, predict where Alice is going to need to stand
and go straight there.

Slash with the sword when you can, and run when you hear the sound effects
that indicate it fired its beams.  Make good use of L/R to turn quickly
without taking steps, as this is a big help getting in position to attack the
shoulder without accidentally touching the boss and dying.

This fight is challenging at first because it's a new dress and the general
game controls make it difficult to stay in melee range without getting hurt
yourself.  But the boss becomes doable, and eventually even pretty easy, once
you get the hang of it.  Gameovers restart you from the boss fight, so you
essentially have infinite tries to get it right.
